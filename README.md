# Whistler Blackcomb Trip, January 2027

Planning workspace for a 6-day ski and snowboard trip to Whistler Blackcomb, BC.

**Goal:** bond with the group while enjoying what the Whistler region has to offer.

## Trip at a glance

- **Dates:** Sun Jan 24 to Fri Jan 29, 2027 (5 nights, 4 ski days)
- **Group:** 5 travelers: 1 snowboarder (advanced intermediate), 2 advanced skiers, 2 intermediate skiers
- **From:** SFO, flying nonstop to Vancouver (YVR), then a shuttle or bus to Whistler. No rental car.
- **Gear:** bringing our own
- **Passes:** Epic Local (10-day combined cap), not blacked out for these dates
- **Lodging direction:** two Airbnb units (3BR + 2BR) close to the lifts, about $860 per person, still to be verified. Hotels not yet priced.
- **Budget:** to be agreed by the group
