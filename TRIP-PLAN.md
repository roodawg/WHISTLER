## 1. Trip Goal (LOCKED)

The purpose of this trip is to **bond with the participants** while **enjoying what the
Whistler region has to offer**. Every planning decision — lodging, activities, pace,
grouping, meals — is measured against those two aims:

1. **Bonding** — shared experiences, time together, low friction, everyone included.
2. **Enjoying the region** — skiing/snowboarding at Whistler Blackcomb plus the wider
   area (village, dining, hot springs, backcountry, culture, nature).

If a choice does not serve bonding or regional enjoyment, it is cut (YAGNI).

## 5. Itinerary (draft v2, Sun 1/24 – Fri 1/29/2027, 6 days)

Pace: 4 full ski days (Mon–Thu), arrival evening and departure day off the snow,
shared dinners every night.

| Day | Plan |
|-----|------|
| **1 Sun 1/24 — Arrive** | Morning SFO→YVR nonstop with gear. YVR→Whistler shuttle (~2–2.5 hrs; confirm it takes ski bags). Check in, walk the village, easy dinner. Early night. |
| **2 Mon 1/25 — Whistler Mountain** | Warm-up day. Whistler Village Gondola, Harmony/Symphony area, groomed blues first so everyone settles in; skiers can add steeper runs. Lunch mid-mountain. **Dinner: Araxi** (group splurge #1). |
| **3 Tue 1/26 — Blackcomb + Peak 2 Peak** | Blackcomb via Excalibur gondola, high-alpine bowls, ride the Peak 2 Peak (4.4 km, ~11 min) back to Whistler in the afternoon. Skiers can take advanced terrain and regroup for lunch; the rider has the pace for blues and easier blacks. **Evening: Scandinave Spa**, casual dinner after. |
| **4 Wed 1/27 — Ski, pick your favorite** | Repeat the best terrain from days 2–3 or explore a new area. **Dinner: Crystal Hut snowcat fondue** (adventure dining; or snowshoe tour first). |
| **5 Thu 1/28 — Last ski day** | Final full ski day, early lift for the best snow. Optional Ziptrek Winter Après zip tour or Sliding Centre bobsled in the afternoon. **Farewell dinner: Bearfoot Bistro** (Ketel One Ice Room). |
| **6 Fri 1/29 — Depart** | Sleep in, village breakfast, pack bags, mid-day shuttle to YVR, flight home. |

Specific run choices should be confirmed against the trail map and daily conditions;
the ability mix (two advanced skiers, one advanced-intermediate rider, two intermediate skiers) is the
main constraint on grouping. Whistler has plenty of blue terrain, so the whole group can ski together
in the morning and the advanced skiers can split off for harder runs after lunch, regrouping for après
and dinner. Dinner reservations should be for 5. 4 days of skiing use 4 of the 10 combined Epic Local days.

## 6. Logistics

### Getting there (no rental car)
- **Flight:** SFO→YVR nonstop on Air Canada and United (Flair also listed, bag fees add up). Round-trip fares were about $140–$200 USD in scattered sources; January is a low-fare month. Not a quote — price on Google Flights for 1/24 and 1/29.
- **Ground:** Whistler Shuttle (door-to-village, winter 2025-26 was $120 one way / $216 round trip CAD, 2026-27 fares from ~$125 CAD reported; 15% off if booked by 11/15/2026 and 10% off round trips per the operator) ([Whistler Shuttle](https://www.ridebooker.com/transfers/yvr/whistler/whistler-shuttle)). Cheaper: Skylynx bus ([yvrskylynx.com](https://yvrskylynx.com/)), advertised $32 one way, unverified for winter 2026-27. With 5 people and 5 ski bags plus luggage, get a quote for a private van/shuttle (per-vehicle or per-person) and compare against 5 bus tickets; confirm bag capacity.
- **Rent a car?** Not recommended: village is walkable, free winter shuttle covers Village North, parking is extra, and the Sea-to-Sky Highway in winter needs winter tires/chains and careful driving. A car makes sense only if you want a Vancouver stopover or Squamish/Brandywine excursions. Revisit if you stay outside the village.

### Gear: bringing your own (decided)
- One ski/board bag per person, roughly $45 first bag each way (Air Canada standard fare CAD 45; Alaska roughly similar) = ~$90 per person round trip, unless a fare class or credit card gives a free bag. Fees changed April 2026 and sources disagree, so confirm on the airline site before booking.
- Carry boots on the plane. Check the weight limit (about 50 lb) so boots/helmets packed in the bag don't trigger an overweight fee.
- Pick a shuttle that carries ski bags, and check the lodging has a place to dry and store gear.
- Lift tickets: not needed (Epic Local passes; see §4).

### Lodging (to price for 1/24–1/29, 5 nights)
- Option A — **5 separate hotel rooms in the village** (e.g., Crystal Lodge, Listel, both described as a short walk to the lifts). Most privacy, simplest, priciest per person.
- Option B — **5-bedroom house, or two adjoining condos (e.g. 3BR + 2BR)** with private bedrooms (Airbnb/Vacasa/Whistler.com), ideally Village or Village North (Marketplace Lodge area is a ~10 min walk plus free winter shuttle). Shared living space supports the bonding goal; usually cheaper per person and has a kitchen and laundry.
- Option C — Creekside: not evaluated.
- Whistler.com lists a 2026-27 lodging deal with a dining credit (terms conflict across pages; confirm). No January 2027 rates were found. Nothing priced yet.
- Recommendation to test: B if five private bedrooms and 3+ bathrooms are available within walking distance of a lift; 5-bedroom units are scarce and pricey, so two adjoining units or A may be the realistic choice. Book early.

### Deals
- Flight+lodging packages: Whistler.com and airline-hotel bundles worth a check. Don't limit the plan to a package.
